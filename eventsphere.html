FAQ

Common questions

Is EventSphere free for students?▼
Yes! Students can register and participate in events for free. Colleges and organizers have tiered plans based on the number of events and participants.
How does QR attendance work?▼
Each registered participant receives a unique QR code. Volunteers scan it using any device's camera, and attendance is logged instantly in real-time.
Are certificates verifiable?▼
All certificates have a unique verification ID and QR code. Anyone can verify authenticity instantly at verify.eventsphere.io without any login.
Can we integrate with our college portal?▼
Yes! We offer REST APIs, webhooks, and SSO integration with most major college ERP systems including SAP, Ellucian, and custom systems.
